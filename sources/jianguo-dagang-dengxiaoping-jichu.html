Theoretical Foundation of the Program of National Construction — A Study Companion to the Selected Works of Deng Xiaoping (Jianguo dagang lilun jichu 建国大纲理论基础——《邓小平文选》学习讲话) — Huang Zheng & Huang Hai 黄峥、黄海

Note on authorship. Despite the phrase 建国大纲 in the title, this is not a text by Sun Yat-sen and not his Jianguo dagang 建国大纲. It is a 1983 People’s Liberation Army party-study volume that treats Deng Xiaoping’s theory as the theoretical foundation (lilun jichu 理论基础) of the PRC’s program of national construction (jianguo dagang in its party-state sense), set against the Selected Works of Deng Xiaoping (《邓小平文选》). It belongs on the PRC / reform-era part of this shelf, alongside the republican-era Sun corpus as a later, contrasting stage of the same “建国” theme.

What this is

Huang Zheng and Huang Hai (黄峥、黄海 编) prepared this as an official-style study companion to the first volume of Deng Xiaoping’s Selected Works, issued by the PLA publishing house 战士出版社 in October 1983 (first edition, Nanjing first printing; 74,000 characters; print run 182,000; 157 PDF pages). It reads as party cadre educational material from the early reform era: eleven lectures walking through the main themes of the Selected Works, framed by a “代序” (surrogate preface) on why the Party’s policy shift under Deng was “a correct decision conforming to the necessary course of history.”

The work sits between two poles of recent Chinese history that it constantly references: the Cultural Revolution (文化大革命) and the Gang of Four (四人帮), which it treats as the disorder to be overcome, and the Third Plenum of the Eleventh Central Committee (十一届三中全会) and the Four Modernizations (四化), which it treats as the path forward. It defends Deng’s 1975 rectification (整顿) and rebuts the criticism of Deng / counterattack against right-deviationist reversal-of-verdicts campaign (批邓、反击右倾翻案风), while insisting on preserving Mao Zedong’s historical standing and continuing Mao Zedong Thought.

How it is organized

代序 — 符合历史发展必然的正确决策. Two epochal events from 1975 to the 12th Party Congress (1982): smashing the Gang of Four and ending the ten-year disorder of the Cultural Revolution; and the Third Plenum correcting “Left” errors and beginning comprehensive rectification of the reversal of verdicts. Locates Deng as a veteran revolutionary whose half-century of work serves the cause, in line with historical-materialist respect for the role of outstanding individuals.

第一讲 — 冲破教条主义和个人崇拜的禁锢，重新确立实事求是的思想路线. Breaking free of dogmatism and the cult of the individual; re-establishing the ideological line of seeking truth from facts.

第二讲 — 坚定不移地把党和国家的工作重点转移到经济建设上来. Unswervingly shifting the focus of Party and state work to economic construction.

第三讲 — 从中国特点出发、走中国式的现代化建设道路. Proceeding from China’s particular characteristics to take the Chinese-style road of modernization.

第四讲 — 尊重知识尊重人才，发挥知识分子在四化建设中的重要作用. Respecting knowledge and talent; giving intellectuals their important role in the Four Modernizations.

第五讲 — 改革政治体制和领导体制，建设高度的社会主义民主. Reforming the political and leadership systems; building a high level of socialist democracy.

第六讲 — 在建设高度物质文明的同时，建设高度的社会主义精神文明. Building a high level of socialist spiritual civilization alongside material civilization.

第七讲 — 发展和壮大革命的爱国统一战线. Developing and strengthening the revolutionary patriotic united front.

第八讲 — 建设强大的现代化正规化的革命军队. Building a powerful, modernized, regularized revolutionary army.

第九讲 — 坚持和改善党的领导，加强党的建设. Upholding and improving Party leadership; strengthening Party building.

第十讲 — 维护毛泽东同志的历史地位，坚持和发展毛泽东思想. Safeguarding Comrade Mao Zedong’s historical standing; upholding and developing Mao Zedong Thought.

第十一讲 — 学好《邓小平文选》，为开创社会主义现代化建设新局面而奋斗. Study the Selected Works of Deng Xiaoping well; strive to open a new situation in socialist modernization.

Content anchors (from OCR of the opening lectures)

  • 1975年整顿. Lecture material opens on the year 1975 as “a page of history hard to forget” — grief, long-suppressed hope, and political storms. It describes how Mao, with Zhou Enlai ill, had Deng preside over daily central work, and how Deng made rectification (整顿) his guiding work principle — first the army, then industry, agriculture, commerce, culture-education, science-technology, and Party organization — fighting the Gang of Four “tit-for-tat but with strategy.” Eight of Deng’s pieces from this period are in the Selected Works.
  • 批邓、反击右倾翻案风. The material recounts how Mao could not tolerate Deng systematically correcting Cultural Revolution errors and launched the “criticize Deng, counterattack the right-deviationist reversal-of-verdicts” campaign; the “three big poisonous weeds” (三株大毒草) — documents drafted from Deng’s guiding line such as《论全党全国各项工作的总纲》— circulated widely and, against the Gang’s intent, raised Deng’s prestige; April 1976 events followed.
  • Appraisal framing. Consistently treats 三中全会 as the turn, the Cultural Revolution as the negative pole to be overcome, and Deng’s line as historically necessary and correct — contemporary PLA study-doctrine voice, not an academic history.

Relation to the other sources

This is the first PRC-era source on this shelf. The three republican-era Sun sources (三民主义, 建国方略) present the revolutionary-nationalist program of 1924; this 1983 volume shows how a later party-state study text re-appropriated the vocabulary of 建国 and 现代化 in a Leninist–reform register. The title’s 建国大纲 echoes Sun’s, but the content is entirely post-1949 party history.
