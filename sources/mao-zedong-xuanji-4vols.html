Selected Works of Mao Zedong, Complete in Four Volumes (Mao Zedong xuanji 毛泽东选集) — Mao Zedong 毛泽东

What this is

《毛泽东选集》 (Mao Zedong xuanji, “Selected Works of Mao Zedong”) is the party-edited collection of writings by Mao Zedong (毛泽东), arranged by the periods of the Chinese Communist Party’s history and by date of composition. The four-volume arrangement here follows the standard editorial preface: Volume One holds writings from the First Revolutionary Civil War (第一次国内革命战争时期, the Great Revolution / united front with the Nationalists) and the Second Revolutionary Civil War (第二次国内革命战争时期, the agrarian revolution / Jiangxi Soviet and Long March years); Volumes Two and Three hold writings from the War of Resistance Against Japan (抗日战争时期); and Volume Four holds writings from the Third Revolutionary Civil War (第三次国内革命战争时期, the 1945–49 civil war through the founding of the PRC).

The editorial preface in this file records the standard provenance: earlier regional editions of the Selected Works were unauthorized, disorderly, and textually unreliable, and omitted important pieces; this selection was made chronologically, drawn more broadly to include works not in the local collections, and each piece was reviewed by the author, with some textual corrections and occasional additions or revisions. It notes the collection is still incomplete — because the Guomindang’s destruction of revolutionary documents and the dispersal of materials in the long wars meant not all of Mao’s works (especially many letters and telegrams) could be recovered. Certain once-popular works were excluded by the author’s wish (e.g. 《农村调查》), and others included only in part (e.g. only Chapter 1 of 《经济问题与财政问题》). The edition carries editorial notes (注释) — some of the “题解” kind attached to the first page of a piece, others political or technical, appended at the piece’s end.

The present file is an e-book (EPUB) rendering of the full four-volume set, with clean digital text. It is a large, authoritative-style source: the standard corpus of Mao’s selected writings from 1925 to 1949.

Chapter breakdown (by volume)

Style: English title + (pinyin + 中文) + short gloss of the major pieces. This is a large corpus; the breakdown names the anchor works of each period rather than every nav entry.

Volume 1 — The two revolutionary civil wars (第一卷)

First Revolutionary Civil War period. Opens with Mao’s early class analysis and the peasant movement:

  • 中国社会各阶级的分析 (Analysis of the Classes in Chinese Society, Dec 1925) — “Who are our enemies? Who are our friends?”; analyzes landlord–comprador, middle (national bourgeois), petty-bourgeois, semi-proletarian, and proletarian classes; declares the industrial proletariat the leading force and the peasantry the closest ally.
  • 湖南农民运动考察报告 (Report on an Investigation of the Peasant Movement in Hunan, Mar 1927) — the famous defense of the peasant associations’ revolutionary excesses (“糟得很” vs “好得很”), the vanguard role of the poor peasants, and the “fourteen great deeds” of the Hunan movement.

Second Revolutionary Civil War period. The Jiangxi Soviet and base-area classics:

  • 中国的红色政权为什么能够存在？ (Why Is It That Red Political Power Can Exist in China?) and 井冈山的斗争 (The Struggle in the Jinggang Mountains) — the rationale and practice of the rural base areas and soviet regimes.
  • 关于纠正党内的错误思想 (On Correcting Mistaken Ideas in the Party) — the Gutian Resolution, correcting purely military viewpoints, ultra-democracy, subjectivism, individualism, roving-rebel ideas, etc.
  • 星星之火，可以燎原 (A Single Spark Can Start a Prairie Fire), 反对本本主义 (Oppose Book Worship — “no investigation, no right to speak”), and the economic/land pieces (必须注意经济工作, 怎样分析农村阶级, 我们的经济政策).
  • 论反对日本帝国主义的策略 (On Tactics Against Japanese Imperialism, Dec 1935) — the Wayaobao speech on the national united front against Japan.
  • 中国革命战争的战略问题 (Problems of Strategy in China’s Revolutionary War) — the strategic manual of revolutionary war.
  • 实践论 (On Practice) and 矛盾论 (On Contradiction, 1937) — Mao’s two great philosophical essays, on the unity of knowledge and action and the law of contradiction, framing the party’s method.

Volume 2 — The War of Resistance, upper (第二卷)

The opening of the war against Japan:

  • 反对日本进攻的方针、办法和前途 and 为动员一切力量争取抗战胜利而斗争 — the initial policy of total resistance.
  • 论持久战 (On Protracted War, 1938) — the centerpiece strategy piece: refutes both the “national subjugation” thesis and the “quick victory” thesis; the three stages of the war; guerrilla and mobile war; “soldiers and civilians are the foundation of victory.”
  • 抗日游击战争的战略问题 (Problems of Strategy in Guerrilla War Against Japan).
  • 中国共产党在民族战争中的地位, 统一战线中的独立自主问题, 战争和战略问题 — party role, independence within the united front, and the primacy of armed struggle.
  • 中国革命和中国共产党 (The Chinese Revolution and the Chinese Communist Party, 1939) — the survey of Chinese society and the nature of the Chinese revolution.
  • 新民主主义论 (On New Democracy, 1940) — Mao’s systematic statement of “New Democracy”: a new-democratic political, economic, and cultural program distinct from both bourgeois dictatorship and socialism, and the assertion that China’s revolution is part of the world proletarian-socialist revolution.
  • 纪念白求恩 (In Memory of Norman Bethune) and 《共产党人》发刊词 — the party-building pieces.

Volume 3 — The War of Resistance, lower (第三卷)

The rectification (整风) and later-war period:

  • 改造我们的学习 (Reform Our Study), 整顿党的作风 (Rectify the Party’s Style of Work), 反对党八股 (Oppose Stereotyped Party Writing) — the core of the 1942–44 Yan’an Rectification Movement against subjectivism, sectarianism, and stereotyped writing.
  • 在延安文艺座谈会上的讲话 (Talks at the Yan’an Forum on Literature and Art, 1942) — the definitive statement of art serving politics and the masses.
  • 学习和时局 (Study and the Current Situation, 1944), 为人民服务 (Serve the People) — the famous commemorative piece on Zhang Side.
  • 抗日时期的经济问题和财政问题 and the production-campaign pieces (组织起来, 必须学会做经济工作, 游击区也能够进行生产).
  • 论联合政府 (On Coalition Government, 1945) — the 7th Congress political report, calling for a democratic coalition government.
  • 愚公移山 (The Foolish Old Man Who Removed the Mountains) — the 7th Congress closing speech, the “two big mountains” of imperialism and feudalism.

Volume 4 — The Third Revolutionary Civil War (第四卷)

The 1945–49 civil war through the founding of the PRC:

  • 抗日战争胜利后的时局和我们的方针 (The Situation and Our Policy After the Victory in the War of Resistance Against Japan, Aug 1945) — policy after Japan’s surrender; the role of Soviet entry in ending the war.
  • 和美国记者安娜·路易斯·斯特朗的谈话 (Talk with the American Correspondent Anna Louise Strong, Aug 1946) — the “paper tiger” thesis (帝国主义和一切反动派都是纸老虎).
  • 集中优势兵力，各个歼灭敌人 and the campaign-guidance pieces for the Liaoshen, Huaihai, and Pingjin campaigns (辽沈/淮海/平津战役作战方针).
  • 目前形势和我们的任务 (The Present Situation and Our Tasks, 1947) — the Ten Great Military Principles and the new democratic economic program.
  • 在中国共产党第七届中央委员会第二次全体会议上的报告 (Report to the Second Plenary Session of the Seventh Central Committee, Mar 1949) — the transition from countryside to cities, from revolution to construction.
  • 论人民民主专政 (On the People’s Democratic Dictatorship, Jun 1949) — the theoretical statement of the new state, summing up 28 years of experience and the “lean to one side” line toward the Soviet camp.
  • 丢掉幻想，准备斗争 (Cast Away Illusions, Prepare for Struggle), 别了，司徒雷登 (Farewell, Leighton Stuart), 为什么要讨论白皮书？, “友谊”，还是侵略？, 唯心历史观的破产 — the late-1949 polemics against the US “China White Paper” and Acheson’s account of modern China, defending the historical-materialist interpretation of the revolution.

Relation to the other sources

This is the first source by the leader of the Chinese Communist Party, and the chronological companion to the Nationalist-side materials:

SourceSide / angle
三民主義, 建国方略, 孫逸仙傳記, 大革命家孙逸仙Sun Yat-sen’s program and lives
蒋介石日记 (1915–1949)The Nationalist leader’s own reflections (war, civil war, 1949)
毛泽东选集 (this file)The Communist leader’s own writings (1925–1949)
建国大纲理论基础 (1983)PRC-era party study re-appropriating the 建国 vocabulary under Deng

Where 蒋介石日记 gives Chiang’s first-person framing of the war, civil war, and defeat, 毛泽东选集 gives Mao’s first-person program, strategy, and theory on the other side — the two leaders’ texts as a matched pair across the 1925–49 revolutionary era, both carried forward in this wiki.
