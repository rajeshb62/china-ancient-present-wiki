Deng Xiaoping: A Record of His Life (Deng Xiaoping shilu 邓小平实录), 1904–1997, complete in four volumes — ed. Li Xinzhi 李新芝

What this is

《邓小平实录》(1904—1997) (Deng Xiaoping shilu, “Deng Xiaoping: A Record of His Life”) is a four-volume biography of Deng Xiaoping (邓小平) covering his entire life, 1904–1997, edited by 李新芝 (Li Xinzhi) and published by 北京联合出版公司 (Beijing United Publishing Company). The edition is the 改革开放40周年纪念版 (“40th Anniversary of Reform and Opening” commemorative edition, 2018), issued in four physical volumes.

The work is organized as a period-by-period life record. Each of the ten parts (编) that span the four volumes is divided into recurring subsections: 历程 (course / career — the chronological narrative), 交往 (relations — Deng’s dealings with key figures), and 珍闻 (anecdotes — vignettes from the period). This makes it a biographical chronicle built from career narrative, relationship portraits, and illustrative episodes, in the voice of a PRC biographical compendium.

The four volumes and their year spans:

  1. 邓小平实录1：1904—1945 (early life through the War of Resistance)
  2. 邓小平实录2：1945—1966 (civil war through the early PRC and the general-secretary years)
  3. 邓小平实录3：1966—1982 (the Cultural Revolution decade and the return to power / rectification)
  4. 邓小平实录4：1982—1997 (the “chief architect” years and old age)

Chapter breakdown (by part)

Style: English title + (pinyin + 中文) + multi-line gloss of the period’s content. The names below are the article/entry titles as they appear in the volume table of contents.

第一编 — Early years (早年岁月, 1904–1927)

历程 (course). From his native 古城广安 (Guang’an), his ancestors’ traces, and his parents, through his 少年时光 (youth), 走出夔门 (leaving Sichuan through the Kuimen gorge), the hard life in France (在法国的艰苦生活) during his work-study years, joining the Chinese Communist Youth League in Europe (参加旅欧中国少年共产党), study at Moscow Sun Yat-sen University (在莫斯科中山大学), and service as political department head at the Zhongshan Military School (中山军事学校政治处长).

交往 (relations). Early relationships: with Zhou Enlai (视若兄长—与周恩来), his two mothers, his teacher Wang Yunsong, Li Fuchun and Cai Chang, Feng Yuxiang, and Zhang Xiyuan (his first wife; 人世间的真情—与张锡瑗).

珍闻 (anecdotes). 第一次远洋 (the first ocean voyage), his birthday in distress, his lively temperament, the Paris café, his love of football, and the blue-and-white-striped scarf.

第二编 — The Red Army period (红军时期, 1927–1937)

历程. 进入首脑机关 (entering the central organs), participating in the August 7th Meeting (参加八七会议), serving as Party Central secretary-general (党中央秘书长), becoming a 25-year-old political commissar, the advance into Jingxi (进军靖西), leading the agrarian revolution (主持土地革命), the Pingma military reorganization (指导平马整训), the thousand-mile campaigns (千里转战), entering southern Jiangxi under emergency orders, serving as the first “official” of the Red capital (首任红都”京官”), the “Deng, Mao, Xie, Gu” affair (“邓、毛、谢、古”事件), the “Red Star” newspaper, the Long March (长征途中), and arriving in northern Shaanxi.

交往. With Mao Zedong (毛泽东的功绩是第一位的—与毛泽东), Wang Jiaxiang, Luo Ronghuan, Zhang Yunyi, Li Mingrui, Wei Baqun, and He Jinnian.

珍闻. Leading the singing of the Internationale, curing illness with pumpkin, escorting Comrade Deng, the lamplight of the Kuixing Tower, the friendship of the Youjiang, the battle at Meihua, and eliminating evil-doers.

第三编 — Taking command in Taihang (立马太行, 1937–1945)

历程. 奔赴抗日战场 (rushing to the anti-Japanese battlefield), establishing the mobilization committees, upholding independence and self-reliance (坚持独立自主), leading the resistance and the anti-reactionary struggles, serving in the Hundred Regiments Offensive (在百团大战中), inspecting the Taiyue and Zhongtiao regions, becoming secretary of the Taihang sub-bureau (出任太行分局书记), presiding over the Wencun Conference, and leading base-area construction (领导根据地建设).

交往. The fifty-year comradeship with Liu Bocheng (战友情谊五十年—与刘伯承), and dealings with Peng Dehuai, Xu Xiangqian, and Li Da.

珍闻. Vignettes of the Taihang base-area years.

第四编 — The Central Plains campaigns (逐鹿中原, 1945–1949)

历程. The civil-war campaigns: the struggle for the Central Plains and the advance to the Dabie Mountains with Liu Bocheng, and the run-up to the decisive campaigns that led to the founding of the PRC.

第五编 — The early PRC (新中国成立初期, 1949–1956)

历程. The founding years: the southwest region (Chongqing and the Second Field Army’s governance of the southwest), the establishment of the new state, and the consolidation of the People’s Republic in its early period. (Sections include his role in leading the southwest and the early state’s recovery and transformation.)

第六编 — Ten years as General Secretary (十年总书记, 1956–1966)

历程. Deng’s decade as General Secretary of the CPC Central Committee (1956–1966): the Eighth Party Congress, the Great Leap era, the adjustment years, and his role in party and state leadership before the Cultural Revolution.

第七编 — Ten years of peril (十年危难, 1966–1976)

历程. The Cultural Revolution decade: Deng’s fall, his exile, the period of peril, and his rehabilitation — including the 1973 restoration and his 1975 comprehensive rectification work before the “Criticize Deng, Counterattack the Right-Deviationist Reversal of Verdicts” campaign.

第八编 — Setting things right (拨乱反正, 1976–1982)

历程. After the fall of the Gang of Four (1976): Deng’s full return, the Third Plenum of the Eleventh Central Committee (十一届三中全会) and the shift to reform and opening, the reversal of unjust verdicts, and the restoration of the party line — the decisive turn of the reform era.

第九编 — The chief architect (总设计师, 1982–1989)

历程. Deng as the “chief architect” (总设计师) of reform and opening: the 12th, 13th, and 14th Party Congresses, the establishment of socialism with Chinese characteristics, the special economic zones, and the deepening of the reform program into the late 1980s.

第十编 — Late-life feelings (晚年情怀, 1989–1997)

历程. Deng’s later years (1989–1997): the post-1989 consolidation, the 1992 southern tour (南巡) and its call to speed up reform, the establishment of the socialist market economy, and his final years, closing with his death in 1997.

Relation to the other sources

Where the PRC history gives the institutional survey and the 1983 study gives Deng’s theory, 邓小平实录 gives the man himself — from the French work-study youth and the Long March, through the Taihang and Central Plains campaigns, the Cultural Revolution ordeal, the return to power, and the reform era he designed. Together these complete the PRC half of the shelf around its central leader.
