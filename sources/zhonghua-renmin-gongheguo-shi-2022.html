History of the People’s Republic of China, 2nd ed. (2022) (Zhonghua Renmin Gongheguo shi 中华人民共和国史) — Editorial Board

What this is

《中华人民共和国史》(第2版) is the official PRC-history textbook of the 马克思主义理论研究和建设工程 (“Marxism Theory Research and Construction Project,” the 马工程 key-textbook program), published by 高等教育出版社 (Higher Education Press) in 2022. It is the project’s standard national key textbook (重点教材) on PRC history, authored by the book’s editorial committee (《中华人民共和国史》编写组). This is the 2nd edition (第2版), revised through the era of Xi Jinping and the achievement of 建成小康社会 (building a moderately prosperous society) and the centenary of the CPC (2021).

The present file is the 2022 second-edition PDF — the document previously attempted in this session as a 150-byte 502-error failed download. It has now been re-fetched as a valid 522-page PDF.

What years does it cover?

1949 → 2021. The book opens with the founding of the PRC and closes with the centenary of the CPC (2021) and the achievement of a moderately prosperous society (全面建成小康社会). Its final substantive topics include the COVID-19 pandemic (2020), the “十三五” plan completion and the “十四五” plan and 2035 vision goals, the all-out victory in the poverty-alleviation campaign (脱贫攻坚), and the 2021 centenary — i.e. it runs into the 2020s, as the user requested. (The book’s own periodization divides the PRC’s “70-some years” into six stages.)

Chapter breakdown

Style: English title + (pinyin + 中文) + multi-line gloss of the chapter’s sections. The section titles below are as they appear in the book’s table of contents.

导论 — Introduction

Frames the study of PRC history: what 新中国史 is, its periodization, and the course from the Opium War and the failure of old-style revolutions through the New Democratic Revolution to the founding of the PRC — the standard official narrative of “the Chinese people stood up, became prosperous, and grew strong.”

Chapter 1 — The birth of the PRC and the establishment of the socialist system (1949–1956)

中华人民共和国的诞生和社会主义制度的确立. From the founding of the state through the completion of socialist transformation.

  • 中华人民共和国的成立 (the founding: New Democratic Revolution’s victory as the basis; the CPPCC and the Common Program; the founding ceremony and the formation of the central government; “opening a new epoch in Chinese history”)
  • 民主革命任务的完成和新生人民政权的巩固 (completing democratic revolution & consolidating power: unification of the mainland, confiscating comprador capital, counter-revolutionary suppression, land reform, democratic reform, and the War to Resist US Aggression and Aid Korea)
  • 国民经济的迅速恢复 (rapid economic recovery: stabilizing prices & unifying finance, adjusting industry & commerce, the “Three-Anti” and “Five-Anti” movements)
  • 过渡时期总路线的提出和社会主义基本经济制度的建立 (the General Line for the Transitional Period; the First Five-Year Plan; the socialist transformation of agriculture, handicrafts, and capitalist industry & commerce)
  • 社会主义政治制度的确立和发展 (the First NPC and the 1954 Constitution; the multi-party cooperation & political consultation system; regional autonomy; defense modernization)
  • 文教科技卫生等事业的建设和进步 (advances in culture, education, science, and health)

Chapter 2 — The arduous exploration and tortuous development of socialist construction (1956–1978)

社会主义建设的艰辛探索和曲折发展. The twenty-plus years of construction under Mao, including both achievement and error.

  • Covers the 8th Congress and the early exploration; the Great Leap Forward and the People’s Communes; the adjustment years; the ten years of achievement and the Cultural Revolution; and the period through the mid-1970s
  • Its sections trace the socialist construction, the setbacks of the “Left” errors, and the defense of sovereignty against “great-power chauvinism” (e.g. the Moscow conference and the Sino-Soviet rift over the long-wave radio station and joint fleet proposals of 1958)

Chapter 3 — Reform & opening and blazing a path of socialism with Chinese characteristics (1978–1992)

改革开放和开创有中国特色社会主义. The turn to reform and the initial establishment of the reform program.

  • 在徘徊中前进 (advancing amid hesitation: exposing the Gang of Four, Deng Xiaoping’s return to leadership, restoring the college entrance examination, the Fifth NPC)
  • 中共十一届三中全会开启改革开放新时期 (the Third Plenum of the 11th Central Committee opens the new era of reform: the criterion-of-truth debate, the great historic turn, the Four Cardinal Principles, economic adjustment)
  • 全面拨乱反正与改革开放的初步展开 (full rectification & the initial reform: rehabilitating the wrongly-accused, the trials of the Lin Biao and Jiang Qing counter-revolutionary cliques, the Historical Resolution, the start of rural reform, the four special economic zones — Shenzhen, Zhuhai, Xiamen, Shantou)
  • 建设有中国特色社会主义的提出和部署 (the 12th Party Congress and building a moderately prosperous society as the goal; the 1982 Constitution; the “Sixth Five-Year Plan”)
  • 改革开放的全面展开与社会主义精神文明建设的推进 (deepening reform: rural reform & the rise of township enterprises, the economic-system reform program, the 14 open coastal cities and the Hainan special economic zone)
  • 社会主义初级阶段理论和党的基本路线的制定 (the 13th Party Congress and the theory of the primary stage of socialism; the basic line; the “three-step” strategy; governance & rectification)
  • 祖国统一大业的推进和国防现代化建设 (“one country, two systems”; the negotiation of the return of Hong Kong and Macao; cross-strait relations; defense modernization)
  • 开启外交工作新格局 (the new diplomatic pattern: the changed judgment of the era’s theme, advances with the US and USSR)
  • 在经受严峻考验中坚持和发展中国特色社会主义 (adhering to socialism through severe tests: quelling the political turmoil of 1989, breaking the Western “sanctions,” governance rectification, the “Seventh” & “Eighth Five-Year Plans”)

Chapter 4 — Socialist modernization facing the 21st century (1992–2002)

面向二十一世纪的社会主义现代化建设. From Deng’s southern tour to the dawn of the new century.

  • Deng Xiaoping’s southern tour (1992) and the 14th Party Congress establishing the socialist market economy as the reform target
  • The 15th Party Congress and the basic program of the primary stage; “Three Represents” (三个代表) thought under Jiang Zemin; the return of Hong Kong (1997) and Macao (1999); responding to the Asian financial crisis and the 1998 floods; the “Nine-Five”/“Ten-Five” plans

Chapter 5 — Advancing toward a moderately prosperous society (2002–2012)

向全面建设小康社会目标迈进. The Hu Jintao era.

  • The 16th Party Congress and the goal of comprehensively building a moderately prosperous society; the Scientific Outlook on Development (科学发展观); the 17th Party Congress and the new demands for that goal
  • The perfecting of the socialist market economy, building a harmonious society, party-governance capacity, the 2008 financial crisis response, the Beijing Olympics, and the completion of the “Eleventh” & “Twelfth Five-Year Plans”

Chapter 6 — Socialism with Chinese characteristics enters a new era (2012–2021)

中国特色社会主义进入新时代. The Xi Jinping era, to the CPC centenary.

  • 全面建成小康社会的战略部署 (the 18th Party Congress and the moderately-prosperous goal; the Chinese Dream of national rejuvenation; the “Twelfth” & “Thirteenth Five-Year Plans”)
  • 统筹推进”五位一体”总体布局 (the “five-sphere integrated” overall plan: economy, politics, culture, society, ecology)
  • 协调推进”四个全面”战略布局 (the “four comprehensive” strategic plan: moderately prosperous society, deepening reform, rule of law, strict party governance)
  • 全力推进国防和军队现代化 (defense & army modernization; the new-era strong-military goals; the revolutionary restructuring of the PLA)
  • 坚持”一国两制”和推进祖国统一大业 (“one country, two systems” & national reunification: keeping Hong Kong and Macao prosperous, promoting cross-strait peaceful development, opposing and containing “Taiwan independence” separatist forces)
  • 推进中国特色大国外交 (major-country diplomacy with Chinese characteristics: the community with a shared future for mankind; the Belt and Road (一带一路); global governance reform)
  • 夺取新时代中国特色社会主义伟大胜利 (the 19th Party Congress and the strategic arrangement for the new era; Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era; the 2018 Constitutional amendment; national security)
  • 在应对风险挑战中推进各项事业 (advancing amid risks: high-quality development; celebrating the 70th anniversary of the PRC (2019); building a world-class army; responding to the COVID-19 pandemic (2020))
  • 加强党的全面领导和提高党的建设质量 (strengthening the party’s overall leadership; party building quality)
  • 全面建成小康社会和开启全面建设社会主义现代化国家新征程 (achieving a moderately prosperous society & opening the new journey: all-out victory in the poverty-alleviation campaign (脱贫攻坚); the “Thirteenth Five-Year” completion, the “Fourteenth Five-Year” plan and 2035 vision goals; the centenary of the CPC (2021))
