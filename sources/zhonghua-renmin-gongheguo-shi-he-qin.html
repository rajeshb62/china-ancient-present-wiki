History of the People’s Republic of China, 2nd ed. (Zhonghua Renmin Gongheguo shi 中华人民共和国史) — ed. He Qin 何沁

What this is

《中华人民共和国史》(第二版) (Zhonghua Renmin Gongheguo shi, “History of the People’s Republic of China,” 2nd edition) is a PRC-era survey history textbook, edited by 何沁 (He Qin) with a collective authorial team (鲁振祥、周鸿、陈明显 等编者), published by 高等教育出版社 (Higher Education Press). It presents a narrative of the People’s Republic from its founding in 1949 through the eve of the 50th anniversary of the PRC — written, as the 前言 states, at the turn of the century to commemorate 建国 50 周年 (1999).

The 内容提要 (abstract) frames the work’s purpose and stance: it aims to “comprehensively and scientifically reflect the tortuous course and brilliant achievements” of the Republic’s first fifty years, clearly narrate and fairly evaluate the major events and major policies of the state, and summarize both the achievements and the positive-and-negative lessons of socialist construction. This second edition added the history after the 14th Party Congress up to the eve of the 50th anniversary, further stressing the theory of building socialism with Chinese characteristics and the building of the socialist market economy. It is an official-party-textbook source: its framing is the CCP’s own account of PRC history, including its treatment of the Cultural Revolution as “errors” (失误) later corrected from the Third Plenum onward.

What years does it cover?

1949 (the founding of the People’s Republic) → 1997 (the 15th Party Congress and the return of Hong Kong). The narrative opens on the preparations for the founding (the CPPCC, the Common Program, the liberation of the mainland) and closes with the 15th Party Congress and the establishment of Deng Xiaoping Theory as the Party’s guiding ideology, with Hong Kong’s return (1997) treated as a “centennial event of the Chinese nation.” The 前言 was written at the 建国50周年 (1999) commemorative moment, so the book’s own temporal horizon is “the first fifty years of the Republic.”

Chapter breakdown

Style: English title + (pinyin + 中文) + multi-line gloss of what the chapter covers, in the book’s own official-party framing.

Chapter 1 — The founding of the People’s Republic (中华人民共和国的建立)

The preparation for the new China. A long prelude traces China’s recent history — from the 1840 Opium War, through the failures of the Taiping, the Hundred Days’ Reform, and the 1911 Revolution, to the May Fourth Movement (1919) and the birth of the Chinese Communist Party (1921), and the long road of the New Democratic Revolution to the eve of 1949. It frames the replacement of the Republic of China by the People’s Republic as the close of the old state.

Sections. The international and domestic conditions at the founding; the convening of the Chinese People’s Political Consultative Conference (中国人民政治协商会议); the drawing up of the Common Program (《共同纲领》); the liberation of the mainland and establishment of local people’s governments; and the formulation of the new state’s foreign policy.

Chapter 2 — Democratic reform and economic recovery (共和国民主改革的全面展开与国民经济的恢复)

The early years of the PRC: the determination of strategic policies at the start of the state, the arduous task of restoring the national economy, land reform, and the nationwide democratic reforms that consolidated the new regime. It also treats the War to Resist US Aggression and Aid Korea (抗美援朝) as part of the early consolidation.

Chapter 3 — The start of large-scale socialist construction and the socialist transformation (大规模社会主义建设的开始和生产资料私有制社会主义改造)

The First Five-Year Plan era: the first National People’s Congress and the first PRC Constitution (1954); the establishment of the state organs; the implementation of regional autonomy for minority nationalities; and the socialist transformation of the means of production (the transition to socialism) with the formation of a highly centralized unified economic system. It includes the ideological-theory education, the publication of volumes 1–3 of the Selected Works of Mao Zedong, the 1956 intellectuals’ conference, and the “Hundred Flowers” / “Hundred Schools” policy.

Chapter 4 — The General Line, the Great Leap Forward, and the People’s Communes (社会主义建设总路线与大跃进、农村人民公社化)

The late-1950s radicalization: the adoption of the General Line of socialist construction, the Great Leap Forward (大跃进), and the People’s Communes (人民公社) movement, followed by the beginnings of correcting the “Left” errors and the convening of the Second NPC. This is the period the book treats as containing serious mistakes in socialist construction.

Chapter 5 — Adjustment and further exploration amid difficulties (在调整政策、战胜困难中进一步探索建设道路)

The early-1960s adjustment: the severe economic difficulties, the formulation of the “eight-character” adjustment policy (八字方针), the comprehensive readjustment of the economy, politics, and culture, the achievements of the decade of construction, the completion of adjustment and the Third NPC, and the defense of national sovereignty against hegemonism with the development of foreign relations.

Chapter 6 — The Cultural Revolution (文化大革命)

The Great Proletarian Cultural Revolution, framed as a period of error and upheaval: the Shanghai “January Storm” and nationwide seizure of power; the “February Adverse Current” and its suppression; the Lin Biao anti-revolutionary group and its abortive coup; the 9th Congress’s legalization of Cultural Revolution theory and practice; the Criticize Lin, Criticize Confucius (批林批孔) campaign; the Jiang Qing group’s failed “cabinet” intrigue and the Fourth NPC; the 1975 comprehensive rectification; and the “Criticize Deng, Counterattack the Right-Deviationist Reversal of Verdicts” campaign. Throughout, the book treats the Cultural Revolution as a grave error later corrected.

Chapter 7 — Economic recovery and the turn of the work focus (国民经济的恢复与全国工作重点的转移)

The post-Cultural Revolution recovery: the restoration of the national economy, the Fifth NPC, the shift of the whole party’s work focus to economic construction, and the formulation of the policy of readjustment, reform, restructuring, and improvement — the opening of the reform era.

Chapter 8 — The rise of reform (农村改革的兴起和城市经济体制改革的启动)

The early reform: the rise of rural reform (household responsibility system) and the start of urban economic-system reform; the preliminary reform of party and state leadership systems; the strengthening of socialist democracy and legality; the 12th Party Congress and the proposal of building socialism with Chinese characteristics; the revised PRC Constitution and the Sixth NPC; the building of socialist spiritual civilization and the party rectification; and the new development of the united front and the multi-party cooperation and political consultation system.

Chapter 9 — The theory of the primary stage and deepening reform (社会主义初级阶段理论和党的基本路线)

The 13th Party Congress (1987) and the theory of the primary stage of socialism and the Party’s basic line; the Seventh NPC and the steady advance of political-system reform; and continued progress amid rectification and deepening reform, the tenth five-year plan and the “Eighth Five-Year” plan, including the fight against bourgeois liberalization.

Chapter 10 — The 14th Congress and the socialist market economy (中共十四大与社会主义市场经济体制)

The 14th Party Congress (1992) and the establishment of the theory of building socialism with Chinese characteristics as the guide for party and state work; the construction of the framework of the socialist market economy and the launching of major reform measures; and the formulation and implementation of the “rejuvenate the country through science and education” and sustainable-development strategies.

Chapter 11 — Hong Kong’s return and the 15th Congress (香港回归与中共十五大)

The return of Hong Kong (1997) as “a centennial event of the Chinese nation”; the 15th Party Congress (1997) and the establishment of Deng Xiaoping Theory as the guiding ideology of the Party. This closes the book’s coverage at the eve of the PRC’s 50th anniversary.

Relation to the other sources

This is the first PRC-era survey history on the shelf — a textbook covering the same post-1949 decades that the other sources approach from specific angles:

SourceCoversAngle
蒋介石日记1915–1949The Nationalist leader’s own view (up to defeat/retreat)
毛泽东选集1925–1949The Communist leader’s own writings
建国大纲理论基础 (1983)1983 party studyDeng-era theory, PRC lens
中华人民共和国史 (this file)1949–1997Full PRC survey in the official textbook voice

Where 毛泽东选集 and 蒋介石日记 end at 1949, and the Deng study is a focused 1983 work, 中华人民共和国史 carries the narrative through the entire post-1949 period to the eve of 2000 — the survey backbone for the PRC half of the shelf.

Source notes

  • Framing caution: This is an official Higher Education Press textbook with the CCP’s own interpretation of PRC history (e.g. the Cultural Revolution as corrected errors, the reform era as the successful path). It is a valuable contemporary-primary framing of the late-1990s, not a neutral academic history.
  • Language: simplified Chinese (mainland, 1999); scanned; OCR medium quality.
  • Not done at ingest: entity / event / concept pages (await Proceed).
  • Category: PRC-era survey history — distinct from the dynastic and republican-revolution sources, and broader than the focused Deng study.
